Inkadra is a free, open source visual workspace for people who make films and photographs:
moodboards, storyboards, shot lists and client review on an infinite canvas, running on your
own server.

- **Boards on an infinite canvas:** notes, images, video, files, links, to-do lists, colours, maps and boards inside boards.
- **Storyboards, shot lists and schedules:** one set of shots, seen as frames, as a table or as shooting days.
- **Together, live:** everyone on a board sees changes as they happen, with comments, roles and an inbox.
- **Client review:** share a board through a link, with comments and picks, and keep some cards for the team only.
- **Your server, your files:** Docker images for amd64 and arm64, S3 or local storage, OIDC sign-in, open export.

> Inkadra is in early development, moving towards 1.0. Try it, and
> [tell us what you think](https://github.com/inkadra/inkadra/issues).
